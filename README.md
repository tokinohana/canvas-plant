# Canvas Plant

**Canvas Plant** was a web-based environmental fundraising platform developed under **Canisius Vanguard SDGs** to support mangrove planting initiatives.

The project aimed to connect digital technology with environmental conservation by providing an online platform where visitors could explore planting projects, contribute donations, and track fundraising progress. The initiative had a fundraising target of **Rp19,000,000**.

## Project Overview

- **Project Name:** Canvas Plant
- **Organization:** Canisius Vanguard SDGs
- **Purpose:** Fundraising for mangrove planting
- **Fundraising Target:** Rp19,000,000
- **Backend:** Django
- **Frontend:** React

## Key Features

- **Online Donations:** Provided an interface for visitors to support mangrove planting initiatives through donations.
- **Planting Projects:** Presented information about environmental projects and their objectives.
- **Fundraising Progress:** Allowed visitors to follow the progress toward the fundraising target.
- **Contribution Leaderboard:** Encouraged community participation by recognizing contributors.

## Project Purpose and Impact

Canvas Plant was developed to support environmental sustainability by making mangrove planting fundraising accessible through a dedicated web platform.

The project demonstrated how web development could be applied to a real-world environmental initiative, combining an interactive user experience with fundraising and community engagement features.

The initiative was associated with a fundraising target of **Rp19 million**. This figure represents the target, not necessarily the amount ultimately raised.

## Background

Canvas Plant was developed as part of **Canisius Vanguard SDGs**, reflecting an effort to use technology in support of environmental conservation and sustainable development.
