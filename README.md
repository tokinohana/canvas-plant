# Canvas Plant

**Canvas Plant** was a web-based environmental fundraising platform developed under **Canisius Vanguard SDGs** to support mangrove planting initiatives.

The project connected digital technology with environmental conservation by providing an online platform where visitors could explore planting projects, contribute donations, and follow fundraising progress. By the conclusion of the initiative, Canvas Plant had helped raise a total of **Rp19,000,000** for its environmental fundraising efforts.

## Project Overview

- **Project Name:** Canvas Plant
- **Organization:** Canisius Vanguard SDGs
- **Purpose:** Fundraising for mangrove planting
- **Final Funds Raised:** Rp19,000,000
- **Backend:** Django
- **Frontend:** React

## Key Features

- **Online Donations:** Provided an interface for visitors to contribute to mangrove planting initiatives.
- **Planting Projects:** Presented information about environmental projects and their objectives.
- **Fundraising Progress:** Allowed visitors to follow fundraising progress throughout the initiative.
- **Contribution Leaderboard:** Encouraged community participation by recognizing contributors.
